# Road to 30 → Road to 13.1

A single-page training dashboard, now in two seasons.

- **Season 1 · Road to 30:** Turkey Trot 5K, Nov 26, 2026 (8-week plan, original badge case).
- **Season 2 · Road to 13.1:** Disney Princess Half Marathon, Sun Feb 28, 2027 (13-week plan, Nov 29 – Feb 28).

The app opens on Season 2 once the Turkey Trot is logged or Thanksgiving has passed. Use the season switcher to go back to Season 1.

## What it tracks
- planned vs. actual workouts for each season
- season mileage, longest run, week streaks, fuel practice
- treadmill, stroller, and solo-outdoor context
- XP and levels that carry across both seasons
- **Challenges:** 12 visible Season 2 goals with progress bars
- **Secret stash:** 11 hidden badges that appear only when unlocked
- unlock toasts when you earn something new

## Skins
Holiday (default through Jan 6, with optional snowfall), Castle (default after), and Classic. Pick one from the header; the choice is saved.

## Data note
Data lives in the browser's local storage under the same key as Season 1, so existing runs carry over. Use Backup/Restore to move or preserve the log.

## Publish with GitHub Pages
In this repository, go to **Settings → Pages**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select **main** and **/ (root)**, then save.
